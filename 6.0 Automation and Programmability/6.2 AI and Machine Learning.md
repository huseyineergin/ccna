# 6.2 AI and Machine Learning
## Introduction
**Artificial intelligence (AI)** uses computers to simulate intelligence, enabling them to exhibit behaviors typically associated with humans, such as recognizing patterns, learning, making decisions and solving problems.

**Machine learning (ML)** is a subset of AI that focuses on enabling computers to learn from data and improve without explicit programming.
- Instead of relying on hard-coded instructions, ML algorithms identify patterns in data and make predictions or decisions based on those patterns.
## Types of Machine Learning
### Supervised Learning
Supervised learning trains a model using **labeled data**, where the correct answer is already known.
- Each input provided to the model for training has a corresponding label.
- The model examines these labeled examples to learn the relationship between the input data and the labels.
- After training, the model uses these learned relationships to classify or predict outcomes for new, unseen data.
- **Advantages**:
	- Highly accurate when labeled data is available.
	- Relatively straightforward to understand and implement.
- **Disadvantages**:
	- Requires large, labeled datasets, which can be expensive and time-consuming to create.
	- Outputs are limited by the patterns and labels represented in the training data.
### Unsupervised Learning
Unsupervised learning trains a model using **unlabeled data**. The model discovers hidden patterns, structures or relationships without being given the correct answers.
- No predefined labels are provided.
- The model identifies patterns, structures and relationships in the data without human supervision.
	- It groups similar data points based on shared features.
- **Advantages**:
	- Does not require labeled data.
	- Reveals hidden patterns.
- **Disadvantages**:
	- The results require interpretation and labeling.
	- The results can be less accurate or more difficult to evaluate.
### Reinforcement Learning
Reinforcement learning trains a model to make decisions by **interacting with an environment** and receiving **rewards** or **penalties**.
- The model learns to take actions that maximize rewards and achieve the best outcomes.
- How it works is:
	- The model, called an agent, interacts with an environment.
	- It takes an action and receives feedback in the form of a reward or penalty.
	- Over time, it learns which actions lead to the best results.
- Applications include:
	- Training self-driving cars to navigate safely through trial and error.
	- Mastering strategies in games such as chess, Go and video games.
	- Teaching robots to walk, pick up objects and perform tasks.
- **Advantages**:Risk of suboptimal learning if the reward system isn’t properly designed.
	- Capable of learning complex behaviors.
	- Adapts to dynamic environments.
- **Disadvantages**:
	- Requires significant computational resources.
	- Can learn suboptimal behaviors if the reward system is poorly designed.
### Deep Learning
Deep learning is a subset of ML that uses multilayer neural networks to learn complex patterns from data for tasks such as image, speech and language recognition.
- An **artificial neural network** is a computational model inspired by how biological neural networks, such as the human brain, process information.
- Data passes through multiple layers of nodes (neurons), with each layer extracting increasingly abstract features.
- Neural networks can be trained using supervised, unsupervised and/or reinforcement learning methods.
- **Advantages**:
	- Excels at processing large, unstructured datasets, such as images, audio and text.
	- Achieves state-of-the-art performance in tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing (NLP) and autonomous driving.
- **Disadvantages**:
	- Requires significant computational resources.
	- The model can be a “black box,” making it difficult to interpret how it arrives at its decisions.
## Types of Artificial Intelligence
### Predictive AI
Predictive AI uses historical data, current information and statistical patterns to estimate what is likely to happen in the future.
- The model identifies patterns and correlations in past data. It applies these learned patterns to make predictions.
- Applications include:
	- Predicting patient outcomes or disease progression.
	- Detecting anomalies that may indicate potential threats or failures.
	- Predicting traffic congestion using historical and real-time traffic data.
	- Forecasting sales trends and customer behavior.
	- Analyzing meteorological data to predict weather conditions.
- **Advantages**:
	- Improves decision-making by providing actionable insights.
	- Identifies potential problems before they occur, such as network issues or severe weather.
- **Disadvantages**:
	- Requires high-quality, relevant historical data.
	- Accuracy depends on how well patterns in past data generalize to new scenarios.
### Generative AI
Generative AI creates new content, such as text, images and computer code, based on patterns it has learned from training data.
- It generates outputs based on patterns and relationships learned during training.
- Applications include:
	- Tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot generate human-like text for tasks such as answering questions, writing code and writing emails.
	- Tools like Midjourney and DALL·E create "artwork" from text prompts.
	- Tools like Sora and Veo 2 generate videos based on user input.
- **Advantages**:
	- Supports creative tasks that would otherwise require significant human effort or time.
	- Enables automation of content creation across various fields.
- **Disadvantages**:
	- Can be misused to create deepfakes or plagiarized content.
	- The quality and reliability of generated content depend on the training data and the model.
	- Can generate entirely incorrect or fabricated answers.
	- Slop.
## Predictive and Generative AI in Networks
Predictive AI:
- **Traffic Forecasting**: Predict network traffic patterns to optimize bandwidth allocation and prevent congestion.
- **Threat Detection**: Identify anomalies or suspicious patterns in real time to mitigate potential security threats.
- **Predictive Maintenance**: Anticipate hardware failures by analyzing historical and current performance data to reduce downtime.

Generative AI:
- **Network Documentation**: Generate documentation about network configurations, policies and other network-related information.
- **Configuration Generation**: Automatically generate configurations for network devices based on desired policies and requirements.
- **Network Design**: Suggest optimized network layouts or modifications tailored to specific business needs and workloads.
- **Troubleshooting**: Generate solutions or diagnostic recommendations based on log files or error messages to resolve issues efficiently.
- **Script Generation**: Automatically generate network automation scripts, such as Python scripts for configuring network devices.
## AI in Cisco Catalyst Center
Cisco Catalyst Center is a centralized network management and automation platform from Cisco that allows IT teams to configure, monitor, secure and troubleshoot enterprise networks through a single interface.
- It features various AI-enabled capabilities to identify issues before they affect users, reduce resolution times and improve network performance and security.

**AI Network Analytics**:
- Uses AI to establish a baseline of normal network behavior.
- Provides insights and recommendations for optimizing network performance.
- Continuously monitors the network to detect and predict anomalies.

**Machine Reasoning Engine (MRE)**:
- Uses AI to perform root cause analysis when network issues arise.
- Suggests resolutions or takes automated corrective actions without manual intervention.
- Reduces downtime by identifying and resolving issues faster than traditional methods.

**AI Endpoint Analytics**:
- Identifies and classifies devices on the network, providing detailed visibility.
- Detects unauthorized devices and unusual behavior.
- Simplifies device onboarding by automating device profiling and segmentation.

**AI-Enhanced Radio Resource Management (RRM)**:
- Optimizes wireless network performance by dynamically adjusting radio settings.
- Uses AI to balance loads, reduce interference and improve coverage across wireless access points.
